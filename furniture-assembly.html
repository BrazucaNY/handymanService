Westchester Furniture Assembly & Anti-Tip Safety Standards

Flat-pack furniture assembly should never take up your entire weekend or compromise your family's home safety. David brings 23+ years of master craftsman experience, complete with industrial assembly tools, precision squaring gauges, and heavy-duty wall anchors.

IKEA PAX & Complex Modular Systems

From multi-frame IKEA PAX wardrobes with sliding glass doors and interior Komplement drawers to Bestå entertainment centers and Nordli modular beds, we build every unit plumb, level, and true to prevent sticking doors and warped frame corners.

Mandatory Anti-Tip Wall Tethering

Protecting children and curious pets is our highest priority. Every tall bookcase, dresser, chest of drawers, and entryway shoe cabinet is securely anchored directly into wall studs using steel brackets or high-strength braided safety cables complying with modern tip-over standards.

Ergonomic Office & Home Furnishings

We assemble motorized sit-to-stand desks (Uplift, Jarvis, FlexiSpot), executive chairs, modular sectionals, dining room sets, and bunk beds. Every foot receives non-scratch heavy-duty felt glides, and all bulky cardboard packaging is collapsed neatly.

Frequently Asked Questions About Furniture Assembly

Do I need to unbox items before you arrive?

No. You can leave all cartons in the room where the furniture will live. David handles careful unboxing, parts inventory audits, hardware sorting, and complete cardboard breakdown upon completion.

What brands of furniture do you assemble?

We assemble furniture from IKEA, Wayfair, Pottery Barn, West Elm, CB2, Crate & Barrel, Amazon, Target, Article, Restoration Hardware, and custom boutique flat-pack manufacturers.

What happens if a piece of hardware is missing?

David carries a comprehensive mobile service kit containing common metric and imperial screws, wooden dowels, cam-lock fittings, and wall anchors to resolve minor manufacturer shortages on the spot.

How do you quote furniture assembly jobs?

We provide transparent upfront project quotes with zero hidden surprises. Send a link, photo, or model number of the items you need assembled for a fast, straightforward estimate.
