Frequently Asked Questions

Do you assemble IKEA furniture?

Yes - we specialize in IKEA furniture assembly. We're experienced with all IKEA products including beds, wardrobes, dressers, bookshelves, and complex systems like PAX and BESTÅ.

What brands do you assemble?

We assemble all major flat-pack furniture brands including IKEA, Wayfair, Amazon Basics, Target, Walmart, Pottery Barn, West Elm, CB2, Article, and more.

Do you offer same-day furniture assembly?

Yes - we frequently offer same-day furniture assembly throughout Westchester County. Call (516) 350-0801 to check availability for urgent assembly needs.

Can you assemble trundle beds, pergolas, and gazebos?

Absolutely. We assemble trundle beds, daybeds, outdoor pergolas, gazebos, playsets, and other complex outdoor structures. These require additional time and are priced accordingly.

Is there a weight limit for furniture you assemble?

There's no weight limit for the furniture we assemble - we handle everything from small nightstands to large entertainment centers and wardrobes. We ensure all items are properly anchored and secured.

How much does furniture assembly cost?

Furniture assembly is priced per job based on the specific items and complexity. Contact us for a free, upfront quote tailored to your exact pieces.
