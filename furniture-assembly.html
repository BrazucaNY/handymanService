DIY vs. Calling Here Handyman

Is flat-pack assembly worth your Saturday afternoon?

🛠️ When You Can DIY:

Simple 2-step nightstands, small coffee tables, or basic office chairs with pre-installed hardware are straightforward DIY builds with basic allen wrenches.

⭐ When to Call Here Handyman:

Complex 6-drawer dressers, IKEA PAX wardrobe systems, large trampolines, or heavy wooden pergolas require 100+ hardware pieces, power drivers, and anti-tip wall anchoring. David completes in 2 hours what takes homeowners full weekend days.
