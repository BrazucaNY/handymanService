DIY vs. Hiring Here Handyman

Should you set up smart devices yourself?

🛠️ When You Can DIY:

Battery-powered indoor plug-in cameras or wireless smart plugs are simple DIY setups requiring only your phone and home Wi-Fi password.

⭐ When to Call Here Handyman:

Wired doorbells, outdoor floodlight cameras into brick/siding, or thermostats without C-wires require drilling, masonry anchors, and low-voltage electrical work. Calling David ensures clean mounting without damaging exterior walls.
