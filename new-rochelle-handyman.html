Serving New Rochelle, NY

New Rochelle is Westchester's second-largest city, spread along the Long Island Sound with beaches, parks, and a long waterfront from Glen Island to Davenport Neck. The downtown, anchored by New Roc City and the Metro-North New Haven Line station, has been transformed by new apartment construction in recent years.

Just beyond downtown, the city keeps its history. The Huguenot Historic District, Thomas Paine Cottage, and St. Paul's Church National Historic Site anchor neighborhoods of older homes, while Echo Bay, Beechmont, and Wykagyl offer everything from seaside cottages to grand pre-war houses.
