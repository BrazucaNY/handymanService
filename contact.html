Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I receive a reply?

David monitors inquiries throughout the business day and typically replies within 30–60 minutes. For immediate urgent assistance, calling or texting (516) 350-0801 is recommended.

How does your pricing work?

We provide transparent flat-rate quotes based on the specific scope of your project. You will always know the exact price before work begins, with zero surprise fees or hidden add-ons.
