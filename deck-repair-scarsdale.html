Scarsdale Deck Repair & Carpentry Services

We solve the structural, safety, and cosmetic issues that plague Westchester outdoor living spaces.

Rotten Board & Joist Repair

Moisture and freeze-thaw cycles deteriorate soft wood. We surgically extract decayed boards, sister compromised joists, and seal ledger boards to keep your foundation rock solid.

Composite Decking Resurfacing

Tired of splinters and yearly painting? We replace aging wood surfaces with Trex, TimberTech, or Azek composite decking with hidden fastener clips for a seamless, barefoot-friendly deck.

Railing & Baluster Stabilization

Wobbly handrails fail building inspections and risk child safety. We re-anchor posts with heavy structural carriage bolts, reinforce stair stringers, and replace weathered balusters.

Stair Stringer & Step Rebuilding

Deck stairs carry heavy foot traffic. We build custom pressure-treated stringers, level out sinking footings, and install slip-resistant treads that withstand harsh winter weather.

Deep Cleaning & Premium Staining

Restore natural wood grain with our controlled pressure washing, wood conditioner treatment, and premium oil-based or acrylic solid stains that protect against UV fading and mold.

Flashing & Hardware Replacement

Corroded galvanized joist hangers, rusted lag screws, and compromised house flashing are replaced with marine-grade exterior fasteners and weatherproof Z-flashing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from Scarsdale homeowners regarding deck repairs and restoration.

Can you replace rotten deck boards without rebuilding my entire deck in Scarsdale?

Yes! We specialize in targeted deck board replacement, structural joist sistering, and ledger board repairs. If your framing is sound, we replace damaged cedar, pressure-treated, or composite boards seamlessly without the cost of a full rebuild.

Do you repair wobbly deck railings, posts, and stairs in Scarsdale?

Yes. Loose deck railings and shifting stairs are safety hazards. We re-anchor posts with heavy-duty structural carriage bolts, reinforce stringers, and replace loose balusters to ensure full building code compliance.

Can you upgrade an older wood deck to modern composite decking like Trex or TimberTech?

Absolutely. We inspect your existing foundation and joists, repair any framing damage, and install premium low-maintenance composite deck boards and hidden fasteners for a clean, splinter-free luxury finish.
