Our 4-Step Invisible Patching Process

01

Clean Cutout & Solid Backing

Damaged, loose, or water-softened sheetrock is scored squarely. We secure solid lumber backing or metal drywall clips behind the opening so the new patch can never flex, cave in, or push inward.

02

Fitted Sheetrock & Mesh Tape

A new piece of matching-thickness drywall (1/2" or 5/8") is custom cut and screw-fastened tightly. High-strength self-adhesive fiberglass mesh tape binds the joints to eliminate future hairline cracks.

03

Multi-Coat Feathered Mudding

We apply rapid-setting joint compound followed by ultra-smooth finishing mud. Each coat is feathered out 8 to 12 inches past the damaged zone, eliminating the visible ridge that amateurs leave behind.

04

Low-Dust Sanding & Paint Match

The repair is hand-sanded glass-smooth with fine abrasive sponges, wiped clean, primed with stain-blocking primer, and touched up with matching wall paint so the repair disappears entirely.

Drywall Repair FAQs

Can you match the texture and color of my existing wall?

Yes. We carefully match smooth level 4/5 finishes, light orange peel, knockdown, and hand-troweled textures. If you have leftover wall paint from your original painting project, we will apply the finish coats so the repair is completely undetectable. If paint is not available, we can cut a small sample swatch for exact color matching at your local paint center.

How do you handle water-damaged ceiling drywall?

Before closing any water-damaged area, we ensure the active plumbing or roof leak is completely resolved and the cavity is dry. We remove all water-logged or mold-compromised gypsum board, treat the joists if needed, install moisture-resistant drywall, and apply stain-blocking bonding primer to prevent bleed-through rings.

What is your pricing policy for drywall repairs?

We provide clear, upfront flat-rate quotes tailored to the exact size, number of holes, and surface height of your repairs. We never charge hidden add-ons or surprise fuel surcharges.

How long does a typical drywall patch repair take?

Most small-to-medium drywall patches (such as doorknob holes or ceiling access cutouts) are completed in a single visit using premium fast-setting hot mud, followed by fine finishing. Larger multi-room or ceiling repairs with painting can be coordinated smoothly with zero disruption to your daily routine.
