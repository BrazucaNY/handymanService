Serving Hartsdale, NY

Hartsdale is an unincorporated hamlet within the Town of Greenburgh, hugging the Central Avenue corridor between Scarsdale and White Plains. It's a commuter community through and through: the Metro-North Hartsdale station serves the Harlem Line, and thousands of residents make the daily trip to Manhattan.

The hamlet is dense with garden apartments, co-op buildings, and modest single-family homes, and it's also home to the Hartsdale Pet Cemetery — the oldest pet cemetery in the United States. High tenant turnover creates constant demand for fast, reliable handyman work: furniture assembly, TV mounting, and between-lease repairs.
