Serving Scarsdale, NY

Scarsdale is consistently ranked among the most affluent communities in the United States, known for its top-ranked school district and its early 20th-century architecture. The village center at Popham Road, a short walk from the Metro-North Harlem Line station, anchors a community of homeowners who care deeply about the condition of their houses.

The village divides into distinct sections — Fox Meadow, Heathcote, Greenacres, Edgewood, and the village center itself — each filled with large Tudors, Colonials, and a handful of Victorians featuring original woodwork, plaster walls, and slate roofs. Those materials age beautifully but they need regular, knowledgeable maintenance. Wondering whether you need a handyman or a contractor?
