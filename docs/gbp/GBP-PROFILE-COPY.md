## 2. Services menu

Add each one under Profile > Edit profile > Services. Keep the names plain and searchable.

### 1. Handyman Repair Visit

```text
One visit for a list of small repairs: sticking doors, loose shelves, hardware, trim, caulking, and touch-ups. Send each task with photos and measurements for a flat-rate quote. Serving White Plains and Westchester County. Owner-operated and insured.
```

### 2. TV Mounting and Cable Management

```text
Secure TV mounting on drywall, plaster, brick, or tile with the right bracket and anchors. Includes stud finding, leveling, and compatible low-voltage cable routing where conditions allow. In-wall electrical work is referred to a licensed electrician. Send TV size, mount model, and wall photos.
```

### 3. Furniture Assembly

```text
Assembly for beds, dressers, desks, tables, cabinets, IKEA and Wayfair furniture, entertainment centers, and selected outdoor pieces. Wall anchoring for tall furniture when recommended and the wall allows. Send product links and box count for a flat-rate quote.
```

### 4. Drywall Repair and Patching

```text
Patching for doorknob holes, cracks, anchor damage, and larger openings, including backing, tape, compound, sanding, and primer. Paint and texture matching quoted separately. Active leaks must be fixed first. Send wide and close-up photos with measurements.
```

### 5. Door Repair and Installation

```text
Adjust sticking doors, hinges, latches, and strike plates. Replace compatible knobs and locks, and repair minor casing. Interior door installation when the opening and frame are suitable. Prehung, structural, or fire-rated doors may need a specialist. Send photos and dimensions.
```

### 6. Shelving and Closet Storage

```text
Installation of floating shelves, bracket shelves, closet and pantry systems, garage shelving, and wall organization. Correct anchors selected for drywall, plaster, masonry, or studs and rated for the load. Send shelf dimensions, wall photos, and intended use.
```

### 7. Curtain Rod and Blind Installation

```text
Measure, position, and level curtain rods, blinds, and shades across single or multiple windows. Suitable anchors for drywall, plaster, masonry, or tile. Customer-supplied products welcome. Motorized or specialty treatments may require a specialist. Send window count, heights, and photos.
```

### 8. Mirror and Art Hanging

```text
Safe installation of bathroom mirrors, large artwork, and heavy wall decor. Wall and mounting points checked, hardware matched, and the piece centered and leveled. Large or heavy mirrors may need two people. Send dimensions, weight, hardware photos, and a wall photo.
```

### 9. Caulking and Sealing

```text
Remove failed caulk, clean and dry the joint, and apply a kitchen- or bathroom-rated sealant with proper cure time. Suitable for tubs, showers, sinks, countertops, and backsplash joints. Not a substitute for fixing failed grout, loose tile, or an active leak.
```

### 10. Trim, Baseboard, and Molding

```text
Install or repair baseboards, shoe molding, door and window casing, quarter-round, and transition strips. Small damaged sections repaired where possible. Caulking, filling, paint, and stain matching quoted as part of the scope. Send photos and linear measurements.
```

### 11. Interior and Exterior Paint Touch-Ups

```text
Localized prep and painting for trim, doors, railings, shutters, and small wall repairs. Cleaning, scraping, sanding, and priming as needed. Weather-dependent for exterior work. Widespread rot, lead paint concerns, or full repaints may need a specialist.
```

### 12. Kitchen and Bath Updates

```text
Small upgrades without a full renovation: cabinet knobs and pulls, door adjustment, shelves and organizers, toe-kick and trim repairs, recaulking, drywall touch-ups, and compatible fixture replacement. Countertops, new circuits, and major plumbing need licensed trades.
```

### 13. Seasonal Home Maintenance

```text
Seasonal repair list for Westchester homes: exterior trim, caulking, weatherstripping, screens, door hardware, loose fixtures, and small drywall or paint touch-ups. Book several tasks in one visit. Specialist inspections for roof, electrical, plumbing, and structural systems.
```

### 14. Playset and Outdoor Assembly

```text
Assembly of customer-supplied playsets, gazebos, outdoor furniture, and selected storage products, subject to size and site requirements. Anchoring and installation reviewed for safety. Send product links, box count, and photos of the assembly area for a quote.
```

## 4. Q&A seeds

If the Q&A module is available in your dashboard, post these as questions and answer them as the owner. Upvote the owner answer. If Q&A is unavailable, reuse the same text on the website FAQ and link to it from posts.

### Q1. Do you charge a call-out fee?

```text
No call-out fee within the regular service area. For most jobs I quote flat-rate from photos, measurements, and a clear task list. If a project cannot be priced accurately without an on-site look, we discuss that before any visit. Scope, price, materials, and exclusions are confirmed in writing before work begins.
```

### Q2. Are you licensed and insured?

```text
Here Handyman maintains business insurance and the applicable Westchester County home-improvement credentials for the work it performs. Ask for current proof when you book. Work that legally requires a licensed plumber, electrician, or other specialist is referred out rather than handled outside scope.
```

### Q3. What areas do you serve?

```text
White Plains and communities across Westchester County, including Scarsdale, Harrison, Rye, New Rochelle, Yonkers, Tarrytown, Hartsdale, Greenburgh, Mamaroneck, Larchmont, Bronxville, Eastchester, and nearby areas. Send your town and project details to confirm availability.
```

### Q4. Can you mount a TV on plaster, brick, or tile?

```text
Usually yes, with the right anchors and mounting method. The wall material, TV size and weight, bracket model, and cable-routing plan all affect the install. Send photos of the wall and the mount so the scope and price can be confirmed. In-wall electrical work is referred to a licensed electrician.
```

### Q5. Do you supply materials?

```text
Either way. You can supply fixtures, hardware, shelves, and furniture, or I can include standard materials in the quote. Customer-supplied items should be on site and match the manufacturer requirements. Specialty or custom-order materials may require a deposit, documented in the estimate.
```

### Q6. How soon can you come?

```text
Most non-emergency jobs are scheduled within the same week when the calendar allows. Urgent but non-emergency repairs are fitted in where possible. Active leaks, gas odors, sparking electrical, or structural issues need emergency service or a licensed specialist first.
```

### Q7. Do you handle electrical or plumbing work?

```text
Only within handyman scope: replacing compatible fixtures, adjusting hardware, and similar non-permitted tasks. New circuits, in-wall electrical, gas work, major plumbing changes, and permitted work are referred to the appropriate licensed trade. Tell me the task and I will confirm what is in scope.
```

### Q8. How does payment work?

```text
The written estimate lists the scope, price, materials, exclusions, and payment terms before work begins. Standard repairs are typically paid on completion. Custom materials may require a deposit, documented in advance. You receive a record of the estimate and payment.
```

### Q9. Do you clean up after the job?

```text
Yes. The work area is protected where practical, and job-related packaging and debris are removed as agreed in the estimate. If disposal or hauling is part of the job, it is written into the scope so there are no surprises.
```

### Q10. Can you do a list of small jobs in one visit?

```text
Yes, that is one of the best uses of a handyman visit. Send each task separately with photos and measurements. Grouping small repairs saves a second trip and usually costs less than booking them one at a time. You get one flat-rate quote for the agreed list.
```

### Q11. Do you work with landlords and property managers?

```text
Yes, for suitable repair, installation, assembly, and maintenance tasks. Repeat and multi-unit work can be scheduled in batches. Send the unit count, task list, and access requirements for a scope and quote.
```

### Q12. What should I send for a quote?

```text
A task list, one wide photo and one close-up per issue, measurements, ceiling height, wall type, product links or model numbers, and any access notes such as parking, stairs, or elevator rules. With that, many jobs can be quoted without an on-site visit.
```

### Q13. What if the job is outside your scope?

```text
I will tell you before booking. Some work requires a licensed plumber, electrician, or other specialist. I would rather refer you to the right trade than take on a job outside scope.
```

Upload 3-5 per week. Real work only. No stock images.

### Launch set (10-12)

1. Logo (square, 720x720 or larger, clean background)
2. Cover image (1024x576, a finished local job, not a text graphic)
3. Owner portrait, friendly, at a job or with the van
4. Branded van or vehicle
5. Tools laid out before a job
6. TV mounting before and after pair
7. Drywall patch before and after pair
8. Door or trim repair close-up
9. Shelving or closet installation
10. Furniture assembly in progress
11. Recaulked tub or sink close-up
12. Finished room or clean work area

### Ongoing

- Before, during, and after for every job where the customer agrees.
- Name files plainly: `tv-mount-white-plains-01.jpg`, not a paragraph of keywords.
- Keep faces and house numbers out unless you have permission.
- Add a short caption only where it helps: service and town, no customer name.

### Photo rules

- Do not upload photos of work you did not do.
- Do not add text overlays to job photos.
- Do not upload the same image repeatedly.
- Keep the cover image updated each season.
