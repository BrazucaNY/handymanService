Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an electrician just to replace an existing light fixture?

No. Swapping an existing light fixture, ceiling fan, or switch into an established junction box is a core handyman service. If new line voltage wiring inside walls is required, we can advise you.

How often should smoke detectors be replaced in NY homes?

National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) standards require replacing all smoke detectors every 10 years, regardless of battery replacement. Expiry dates are printed on the back of each unit.

Can you install light fixtures on high 2-story ceilings?

Yes, we bring multi-position A-frame ladders to safely access high foyer lights, staircase chandeliers, and vaulted living room ceilings.
