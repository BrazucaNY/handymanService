Thorough Gutter Cleaning & Downspout Flushing in Westchester County

Your gutters are your home's first line of defense against rainwater infiltration, roofline rotting, and severe basement foundation flooding. Westchester County’s lush oak, pine, and maple tree cover means that gutters accumulate thick organic debris throughout spring blossom and autumn foliage. When gutters become clogged, heavy storm overflow cascades down siding, saturates soil against basement foundations, and rots exterior fascia boards and soffits.

Our Complete 4-Step Gutter Care Process

  • 1. Hand Debris Removal: We clean gutters entirely by hand, bagging decaying leaves, pine needles, acorns, and roof shingle grit rather than blowing wet sludge across your landscape or driveway.
  • 2. High-Pressure Downspout Flushing: We verify clear water flow by snake-testing and power-flushing each downspout and underground leader elbow to prevent hidden blockages.
  • 3. Gutter Pitch & Fascia Inspection: We examine gutter hangers, spikes, and seams for sagging, standing water pitch issues, or loose brackets that cause overflow.
  • 4. Ground & Landscape Clean-Up: All roofline debris is swept, bagged, and hauled away, leaving your walkways, lawn, and gardens spotless.

Stainless Steel Micro-Mesh Gutter Guard Installation

Tired of cleaning gutters twice a year? We install commercial-grade aluminum and stainless steel micro-mesh leaf filters that fit securely over existing 5-inch and 6-inch K-style gutters. These low-profile guards block even the smallest pine needles and roof grit while handling maximum rainfall volume during heavy northeast coastal storms.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should gutters be cleaned in Westchester County?

Most Westchester homes require gutter cleaning twice per year: once in late spring (clearing pollen pods, seed tassels, and blossoms) and once in late fall after all oak and maple leaves drop, before freezing winter temperatures cause ice damming.

Do you clean multi-story and steep roof gutters?

Yes. We are equipped with professional ladder stabilizers, stand-offs, and safety gear to clean standard one-story, two-story, and walk-out basement rooflines safely without denting your aluminum gutter edges.

What is your pricing policy for gutter cleaning?

We provide upfront flat-rate pricing based on your home's perimeter, roofline height, and current tree cover. Zero hidden costs or unexpected surcharges.
