DIY vs. Hiring Here Handyman

Should you mount your TV yourself?

🛠️ When You Can DIY:

Mounting a small 32-inch monitor onto a standard hollow drywall with pre-marked wood studs using a simple fixed bracket is manageable with a level and drill.

⭐ When to Call Here Handyman:

Large 65"+ TVs, fireplace mounting, plaster walls, brick, or in-wall cable routing require lag bolts, heavy-duty masonry anchors, and electrical safety. Calling David prevents costly TV falls or damaged drywall.
