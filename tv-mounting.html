Frequently Asked Questions

What size TVs can you mount?

We mount all TV sizes from 32 inches up to 85 inches and larger. Our mounts are rated for the specific weight of your TV to ensure safe, secure installation.

Can you hide the cables in the wall?

Yes - we offer professional in-wall cable concealment. We run cables through the wall for a clean, professional look with no visible wires.

Do you mount on all wall types?

Absolutely. We mount on drywall, plaster, brick, concrete, and stone. We use the appropriate anchors and mounting hardware for each surface type.

How much does TV mounting cost?

TV mounting is priced per job based on TV size, wall material (drywall, plaster, brick), and cable concealment preferences. Contact us today for a free, upfront quote.

Do you offer same-day TV mounting?

Yes - we frequently offer same-day TV mounting throughout Westchester County. Call (516) 350-0801 to check availability.

Do you provide the TV mount or do I need to buy one?

We can provide professional-grade mounts at competitive pricing, or we can install a mount you've already purchased. Either way, we ensure it's installed correctly.
