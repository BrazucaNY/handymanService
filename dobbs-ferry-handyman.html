Serving Dobbs Ferry, NY

Dobbs Ferry is a small, beloved village on the east bank of the Hudson River, known for its walkable downtown along Broadway and Cedar Street, its arts scene, and its beautifully preserved Victorian and Craftsman architecture. Mercy College's main campus gives the village a youthful energy, while longtime families have lived in the same homes for generations.

Many Dobbs Ferry homes are 80 to 130 years old, which means skilled maintenance and respect for original materials.
