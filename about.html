Meet David

Owner & Lead Technician

I'm originally from Brazil and have lived and worked in different parts of the world before settling in Westchester County, New York.

Before focusing on handyman services, I worked in different fields and gained extensive hands-on experience with home repairs, maintenance, construction, and general problem-solving. Over the years, I developed the practical skills and attention to detail that led me to build Here Handyman.

Experience: Over 15 years of hands-on experience in home repairs, maintenance, carpentry, painting, drywall, plumbing, electrical fixture replacement, furniture assembly, TV mounting, and general handyman work.

Our Story

Here Handyman was built on a simple idea: Westchester County homeowners deserve honest, reliable handyman services without the middlemen and markups of larger companies.

We serve communities across Westchester including White Plains, Scarsdale, Yonkers, Tarrytown, Hartsdale, Harrison, New Rochelle, Greenburgh, Valhalla, Elmsford, Dobbs Ferry, Ardsley, and surrounding areas.

No middlemen, direct expert care — when you hire Here Handyman, you work directly with me, David. I show up on time, communicate clearly, and treat your home with respect.
