Frequently Asked Questions

Is Here Handyman fully insured in Westchester County?

Yes! Here Handyman is fully covered with comprehensive commercial general liability insurance for all residential and commercial home repairs. Certificates of Insurance (COI) are readily provided upon request for condo, co-op, and HOA buildings.

Can I give you a list of 5 to 10 small repair jobs for one visit?

Yes! That is our specialty. We handle multi-item punch lists efficiently in a single visit.

Can you fix interior doors that drag on carpet or stick in the frame?

Yes. We plane wooden door edges, adjust hinge alignment, and reposition latch strike plates.

Do you re-caulk bathtubs, showers, and kitchen sinks?

Yes. We strip old mildewed silicone, clean the joint, and apply fresh 100% waterproof silicone.

How do I schedule a general handyman punch-list visit?
