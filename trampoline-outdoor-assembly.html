Westchester Backyard Play Systems & Storm Anchoring

Trampoline Brand Assembly

We build round, oval, and rectangular trampolines across all major brands including Springfree composite rod systems, Skywalker, Jumpzylla, AlleyOOP, and Acon. We ensure even spring tension, tight safety netting, and secure zipper entrances.

High-Wind Tie-Down Anchoring

Westchester coastal and hilltop storms can easily flip unanchored trampolines and gazebos. We install heavy-duty steel corkscrew earth anchors with high-tensile ratchet tie-downs deep into the turf, or expansion masonry anchors for stone patios.

Pergolas, Gazebos & Playsets

From cedar wood Yardistry pergolas and Backyard Discovery playsets to aluminum hardtop gazebos (Purple Leaf, Sunjoy), we square every post, torque every roof bolt, calibrate swing attachments, and break down all bulky shipping boxes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you assemble Springfree and Skywalker trampolines?

Yes! We specialize in Springfree rod tensioning, Skywalker net enclosures, Jumpzylla, and all outdoor trampoline brands.

Can you anchor gazebos and pergolas to concrete or stone patios?

Yes. We use heavy masonry anchors or ground stakes to secure pergolas and gazebos against wind.

Do you haul away the massive cardboard boxes after assembly?

Yes, full box breakdown and packaging cleanup is included with our outdoor assembly service.

How do I book outdoor assembly in Westchester?

Call or text (516) 350-0801 or book online at herehandyman.com.

What clearance is required around a backyard trampoline?

We recommend a minimum of 3 to 5 feet of lateral clearance away from fences, tree branches, siding, and sheds, along with at least 18 feet of vertical clearance. We check the ground level to make sure the jumping mat stays balanced.

Can you disassemble and move an existing trampoline to a new spot?

Yes. We offer trampoline disassembly, yard relocation, spring re-tensioning, and safety inspection services for families rearranging their backyards or moving to a new home in Westchester.
