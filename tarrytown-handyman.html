Serving Tarrytown, NY

Tarrytown is one of Westchester's most storied villages, immortalized as the setting of Washington Irving's "The Legend of Sleepy Hollow" and home to landmarks like Lyndhurst mansion, the Old Dutch Church, and the Tarrytown Music Hall. Perched on the east bank of the Hudson, it has been a commuter town since the railroad arrived — today Metro-North's Hudson Line station puts midtown Manhattan about 45 minutes away.

The village's housing stock is its defining feature: Victorian, Queen Anne, and Colonial Revival homes, many over 100 years old, alongside renovated carriage houses and a few modern infill builds. Plaster walls, original woodwork, and slate or tile roofs need specialized, gentle maintenance. Our guide to finding a reliable handyman covers what to ask before hiring anyone to work on an older home like yours.
