Serving Tarrytown, NY

Tarrytown is one of the most historically rich villages in Westchester County, immortalized as the setting of Washington Irving's Legend of Sleepy Hollow. Perched above the Hudson River, its streets feature beautiful Victorian, Queen Anne, and Colonial Revival homes - many over 100 years old. Landmarks like Lyndhurst mansion and the Old Dutch Church draw visitors year-round. Many Tarrytown homeowners are remote workers who have invested in older homes and need skilled, trustworthy help maintaining them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you repair plaster walls in Tarrytown's older Victorian homes?

Yes - plaster wall repair is something we handle regularly in Tarrytown's historic homes. We patch, texture-match, and paint so repairs blend invisibly with the original wall.

Do you restore or repair original woodwork and trim in Tarrytown historic homes?

Absolutely. We work carefully with original millwork, baseboards, window casings, and door trim. We understand these details are irreplaceable and treat them accordingly.
