Frequently Asked Questions

How high should a staircase handrail be installed?

In New York building code, stair handrail heights must be between 34 inches and 38 inches measured vertically from the tread nosing to the top of the rail. We ensure exact compliance.

Can you install grab bars on tile or stone shower walls?

Yes. We use specialized diamond masonry drill bits to penetrate porcelain, ceramic, or marble tile cleanly without cracking, and anchor securely into studs or structural backing.

How fast can you complete a handrail installation?

Most standard interior handrail or grab bar projects are completed within 1 to 2 hours with no mess left behind.
