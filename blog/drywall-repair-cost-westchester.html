Why Does Drywall Repair Cost So Much in Westchester?

A proper drywall repair isn't a one-step process. It involves: patching the hole, applying joint compound in multiple coats (each requiring drying time), sanding smooth, applying texture to match the surrounding wall, priming, and painting. Skipping any step results in a visible, bumpy repair that looks worse than the original hole.

Westchester labor rates are higher than much of the state, and the quality of the housing stock — particularly in towns like Scarsdale, Harrison, and Tarrytown — demands high-quality work. A visible patch in a $1.5 million home is not acceptable.

Plaster vs. Drywall: An Important Westchester Distinction

Many of Westchester's older homes — particularly in Tarrytown, Dobbs Ferry, Scarsdale, and Yonkers — have plaster walls, not modern drywall. Plaster repair is more complex and typically costs 20–40% more than drywall repair because the technique is different and plaster is harder to match. Always tell your handyman whether you have plaster or drywall when getting a quote.

Can You DIY Drywall Repair in Westchester?

Small holes under an inch — nail holes, small anchors — are very DIY-friendly with a pre-mixed spackle from Home Depot. Larger holes are harder. The challenge isn't the patching; it's matching the wall texture. Westchester homes have a wide variety of textures — smooth, orange peel, skip trowel, knockdown — and matching them takes experience and the right tools. Most homeowners end up with a visible repair that bothers them for years.

What to Ask Before Hiring for Drywall Repair

  • Does the price include paint? (Some handymen quote labor only)
  • Do you texture-match or just leave it smooth?
  • How many coats of joint compound do you apply?
  • Is the handyman insured in case of further damage?
  • Do you handle plaster walls as well as drywall?
