Move-In Handyman Checklist for Westchester NY

Moving into a new single-family home, co-op, or apartment in Westchester County is an exciting milestone—but unpacking and tackling dozens of mounting, assembly, and fixture repair tasks can quickly become overwhelming. Here is your ultimate move-in handyman checklist.

Phase 1: Day-One Priority Tasks (Safety & Comfort)

Tackle these high-priority items on your first day in your new Westchester residence:
  • Door Lock & Deadbolt Swaps: Re-keying or replacing exterior door locks and smart keypads for complete security.
  • Primary Bed Frame & Mattress Assembly: Assembling master bedroom bed frames and dressers so you can sleep comfortably.
  • Smoke & Carbon Monoxide Detector Testing: Replacing 9V batteries and installing fresh smoke alarms in hallways and bedrooms.
  • Main Water & Electrical Panel Check: Locating and labeling main water shutoff valves and electrical breaker panels.

Phase 2: Week-One Unpacking & Mounting Projects

Once essential furniture is unpacked, focus on functional mounting and organization:
  • TV Wall Mounting & Cable Concealment: Mounting living room and bedroom TVs securely into wall studs with in-wall power hiding.
  • Window Coverings & Blinds: Mounting curtain rods, cellular shades, or venetian blinds for essential privacy.
  • Storage & Wardrobe Systems: Assembling IKEA PAX wardrobes, KALLAX units, Wayfair dressers, and garage shelving.
  • Heavy Mirror & Artwork Hanging: Hanging heavy entryway mirrors, gallery walls, and artwork using wall anchors.

Navigating Westchester Co-op & Condo Move-In Rules

If you're moving into a co-op or condo building in White Plains, Yonkers, or Eastchester (such as Garth Road co-ops), building management typically requires contractor Certificates of Insurance (COI) naming the management board, strict compliance with designated work hours (9 AM – 5 PM Mon–Fri), and elevator floor padding. We handle COI requests seamlessly so your move remains stress-free.
