Fall Gutter Cleaning & Maintenance in Westchester NY

As autumnal leaves fall across Westchester County—from the mature oak trees of Scarsdale to the wooded neighborhoods of White Plains and Rye—clogged gutters become a primary threat to your home's foundation, siding, and roofline.

Why Autumn Gutter Cleaning Matters in Westchester

When autumn leaves, twigs, and pine needles accumulate in your gutter channels, rain runoff backs up under your roof shingles and down fascia boards. During Westchester's freezing winter temperatures, trapped water freezes into heavy ice dams, pushing shingles up and creating costly leaks inside ceilings and walls.

What a Complete Fall Gutter Maintenance Visit Includes

Professional fall gutter maintenance goes beyond scooping out dry leaves. A full service includes:
  • Clearing all roofline gutter channels of leaves, muck, and debris
  • Flushing downspouts with high-pressure water to clear underground drains
  • Inspecting gutter hangers, seams, and drop outlets for sagging or leaks
  • Re-securing loose fascia brackets and re-caulking joint seams

When to Clean Gutters in Westchester County

In Westchester County, the ideal window for fall gutter cleaning is late October through late November, after the bulk of deciduous leaves have fallen but before the first hard ground freeze.
