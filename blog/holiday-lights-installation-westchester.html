Holiday Lights Installation in Westchester NY

Hanging outdoor holiday lights on steep Westchester rooflines, multi-story colonials, and high trees can be dangerous without proper extension ladders, clips, and weather-rated outdoor timers. Let David handle your exterior holiday lighting safely and cleanly.

Why Hire a Professional for Holiday Light Installation?

Every November and December, thousands of emergency room visits occur due to ladder falls while hanging Christmas lights. Westchester homes—with their steep gables, two-story rooflines, and high eaves—present significant hazards during cold, slick winter weather. Hiring a skilled local handyman ensures your lights are mounted securely using professional grade hardware without risking injury or property damage.

Step-by-Step Exterior Holiday Lighting Installation

Our professional holiday display setup process includes:
  1. Pre-Install Strand Testing: Testing all LED light strands and replacing burnt-out bulbs prior to climbing ladders.
  2. Damage-Free Clip Attachment: Securing C9 or C6 bulb strands along fascia and gutter lines using non-invasive gutter/shingle clips—never nails, screws, or staples.
  3. Wreath & Garland Mounting: Securely hanging heavy holiday door wreaths, porch pillar wraps, and window greenery.
  4. GFCI & Timer Integration: Connecting light strands to outdoor GFCI outlets with heavy-duty weatherproof extension cords and setting digital automated dusk-to-dawn timers.
  5. Post-Holiday Takedown & Storage: Carefully taking down lights in January and packing them neatly for next season.

When to Reserve Your Westchester Installation Slot

Holiday light installation slots fill up quickly across Westchester County between November 1st and December 10th. Booking your installation window early in October guarantees your preferred date before peak holiday rush and early winter snowstorms arrive.
