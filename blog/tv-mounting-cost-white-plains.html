TV Mounting Cost in White Plains & Westchester NY (2026)

Published July 15, 2026 • 5 min read

Wall-mounting your television instantly upgrades your living space, giving it a sleek, modern look while freeing up valuable floor space. If you are looking to upgrade your home theater setup, you're likely wondering about the tv mounting cost in white plains ny. Mounting a TV involves more than just drilling a few holes—it requires finding studs, ensuring a level mount, and managing cords. Here is our 2026 pricing guide for TV mounting in Westchester County.

Average Cost to Mount a TV in Westchester

In general, professional TV mounting in White Plains and the surrounding Westchester area costs between $125 and $300. The exact price depends on several factors, including the size of your TV, the type of wall, and whether you want the cables hidden in the wall.

Service Provided Average Cost Range
Basic TV Mounting (Drywall, standard bracket) $125 - $175

How Wall Type Affects TV Mounting Cost

The type of wall in your White Plains home plays a significant role in the cost and difficulty of mounting a TV:

  • Drywall over Wood Studs: This is the most common and standard installation. It is straightforward and typically falls on the lower end of the pricing spectrum.
  • Plaster Walls: Many older homes in Westchester have lath and plaster walls. These require special anchors and extra care to prevent cracking, adding to the labor cost.
  • Brick or Concrete: Mounting a TV on masonry requires a hammer drill and heavy-duty masonry anchors. This takes more time and specialized equipment, increasing the cost.
  • Metal Studs: Common in high-rise apartments and condos in White Plains, metal studs require special toggle bolts, as standard wood screws will strip right out.

TV Size and Bracket Type

The size of your television also dictates the cost. A massive 85-inch TV often requires a two-person team to safely lift and secure the screen to the wall, which will increase the labor rate. Additionally, the type of bracket matters:

  • Fixed / Flush Mount: The simplest and most affordable option. The TV sits flat against the wall.
  • Tilting Mount: Slightly more complex, allowing you to angle the screen down (perfect for mounting above a fireplace).
  • Full-Motion (Articulating) Mount: Allows you to pull the TV out and swivel it side-to-side. These brackets are heavy and require precise stud mounting to hold the torque of a fully extended TV.

Cable Hiding Cost

A beautifully mounted TV can be ruined by a tangled mess of dangling black cords. Cable concealment is a popular add-on service.

External Cord Covers: The most budget-friendly option ($20-$50). Wires run down the face of the wall inside a paintable plastic track.

In-Wall Concealment: This premium look involves dropping the HDMI and power cables behind the drywall and bringing them out near the baseboard. This requires specialized in-wall rated power kits (to meet NY electrical codes) and significantly increases the cost ($100-$200 extra) due to the complexity of fishing wires past insulation and fire blocks.

When to Hire a Pro in White Plains

While some handy homeowners tackle TV mounting themselves, hiring a professional ensures your expensive television won't come crashing down. If you're dealing with plaster walls, brick, a TV over 65 inches, or you want wires hidden cleanly inside the wall, calling a local handyman is the safest bet for a perfect install.
