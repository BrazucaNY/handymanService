Handyman vs Contractor: Which Do You Need in Westchester NY?

Published July 15, 2026 • 6 min read

When you have a home improvement project on your to-do list, knowing who to call can be confusing. Many homeowners in our area find themselves debating: handyman vs contractor in westchester ny? The choice affects the cost, timeline, and legality of your project. Here is a comprehensive guide to help you decide.

What is a Handyman?

A handyman is a skilled "jack-of-all-trades" who handles small to medium-sized home repairs and maintenance tasks. They are perfect for knocking out a punch list of odd jobs around the house in a single visit. Whether you need plumbing repairs, drywall patching, or general home maintenance, a handyman can handle it efficiently.

Typical Handyman Jobs:

  • TV mounting and shelf installation
  • Furniture assembly (IKEA, Wayfair, etc.)
  • Minor drywall patching and painting
  • Replacing faucets or light fixtures (direct swap-outs)
  • Door and window hardware repairs

What is a General Contractor?

A General Contractor (GC) oversees large, complex construction projects. They manage specialized subcontractors (electricians, plumbers, framers) and handle the logistics of major renovations.

Typical Contractor Jobs:

  • Full kitchen or bathroom remodels
  • Room additions or structural wall removal
  • Building a new deck from scratch
  • Projects requiring major electrical or plumbing overhaul

Legal Limits and Permits in New York

In Westchester County, specific towns (like Yonkers, White Plains, and New Rochelle) have strict codes dictating what requires a permit.

Permits: If a job requires a building permit, you usually need a licensed contractor. Moving load-bearing walls, running new electrical circuits, or moving plumbing lines all require specialized, licensed tradesmen and town inspections.

Cost Limits: In some NY jurisdictions, a handyman is legally restricted from taking jobs that exceed a certain monetary threshold for labor and materials without holding a Home Improvement Contractor (HIC) license.

Cost Differences: Handyman vs. Contractor

The cost difference between a handyman and a contractor is substantial, reflecting the scale of their respective projects.

Factor Handyman General Contractor
Pricing Structure Priced per job with clear upfront quote Project-based flat fee (often 10-20% markup on total project cost)
Project Scope Small repairs, 1-2 day projects Large renovations, weeks to months
Subcontractors Usually works alone or with a helper Hires and manages multiple specialized trades

Which Should You Hire?

Hire a Handyman if: You have a list of minor repairs, you need a TV mounted, a door fixed, or you need small cosmetic updates that don't involve moving walls or pipes.

Hire a Contractor if: You are gutting a room, adding square footage, or need a project that requires local town permits and architectural plans.

By choosing the right professional for the job, you'll save money, avoid legal headaches, and ensure your Westchester home is well cared for.
