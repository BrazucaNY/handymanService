What Does Smart Home Installation Include?

A professional smart home installer does more than just mount a device. The real value is in the configuration: connecting the device to your Wi-Fi, setting up the mobile app, testing every function, and making sure it integrates with your existing devices (Alexa, Google Home, Apple HomeKit). A Ring doorbell that isn't properly configured to send motion alerts defeats the purpose.

Do I Need an Electrician or a Handyman for Smart Home Devices?

For most consumer smart home devices — Ring, Nest, smart locks, cameras, smart bulbs — a qualified handyman is all you need. These devices are designed for homeowner installation and don't require a licensed electrician. The exception: if you're installing a whole-home automation system, adding new circuits, or running new wiring, an electrician is required in New York State.

Tips for Smart Home Setup in Westchester Homes

  • Check your Wi-Fi signal strength at the install location before buying any device
  • For older Westchester homes, check that smart switches will have the required neutral wire
  • Buy devices in the same ecosystem (all Amazon Alexa, all Google Home, or all Apple HomeKit) for easier integration
  • Have your Wi-Fi password ready — your installer will need it for every device
  • Consider a whole-home package — installing 5+ devices at once is significantly cheaper than separate visits
