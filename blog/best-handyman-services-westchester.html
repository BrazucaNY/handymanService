What to Look For in a Handyman

A good handyman should be more than just someone who knows how to swing a hammer. They should offer professionalism, transparency, and high-quality work. When searching for the best in Westchester, prioritize these factors:

  • Clear Communication: Do they answer the phone? Do they return calls promptly? Good communication is the first sign of a reliable business.
  • Transparent Pricing: The best handymen will offer clear upfront estimates per project before starting any work.
  • Online Reviews: Check Google and Yelp. Consistent 5-star ratings (like ours!) indicate a track record of happy customers in Westchester.

Licensing and Insurance in New York

In Westchester County, protecting your home is paramount. Never hire a handyman who is not fully insured. If an uninsured worker is injured on your property, or if they accidentally burst a pipe, you could be held liable.

Ask for Proof of Insurance: A legitimate business will gladly provide a Certificate of Insurance (COI) upon request, detailing their general liability coverage.

Depending on the scope of work, they may also need a Home Improvement Contractor (HIC) license issued by Westchester County. Always verify that they are operating legally within your specific town.

Questions to Ask Before Hiring

Before you let anyone start work on your house, ask these crucial questions:

  1. Are you fully licensed and insured in Westchester County?
  2. Do you charge for estimates?
  3. Do you guarantee your work? (e.g., If the TV mount sags, will you come back and fix it?)
  4. Will you be doing the work yourself, or sending a subcontractor?
  5. Are you fully insured in Westchester County?
