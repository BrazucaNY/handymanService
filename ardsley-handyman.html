Serving Ardsley, NY

Ardsley is a small incorporated village in the Town of Greenburgh with a population of around 4,500 and a genuine small-town feel that is increasingly rare in suburban Westchester. The Ardsley Country Club anchors the community, and Ashford Avenue serves as the village's modest commercial main street. Homes here are predominantly 1950s and 1960s split-levels, ranch homes, and Cape Cods that need regular maintenance to stay in top shape. The Saw Mill River Parkway connects Ardsley commuters to the city. In a village where neighbors talk, Here Handyman is the handyman service people recommend.
