Serving Ardsley, NY

Ardsley is a small incorporated village within the Town of Greenburgh, with a population around 4,500 and a genuine small-town feel that's increasingly rare in suburban Westchester. Ashford Avenue serves as the village's modest commercial main street, and the Ardsley Country Club has anchored the community for generations.

The housing stock is predominantly 1950s and 1960s split-levels, ranch homes, and Cape Cods — well-built postwar houses that reward regular, knowledgeable maintenance. There's no train station in the village, so most residents drive; the Saw Mill River Parkway and the nearby Metro-North stations in Hartsdale and Ardsley-on-Hudson serve the area.
