Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about interior painting with Here Handyman.

How long does a standard interior room painting job take?

Most single-room interior painting projects (such as a bedroom, home office, or nursery) take 1 to 2 days. This allows adequate time for drywall patching, sanding, taping trim, priming, and applying two full coats of paint with proper drying time between coats.

Do I need to supply the paint or do you bring it?

We can handle everything! If you already have your preferred color code or paint cans purchased, we are happy to use them. Otherwise, we can purchase premium Benjamin Moore or Sherwin-Williams paints on your behalf and include them in your flat-rate project quote.

Do you repair drywall holes and water stains before painting?

Yes! We never paint over damaged drywall. We repair nail holes, doorknob dents, tape seams, water stains, and stress cracks, sanding the wall glass-smooth before applying stain-blocking primer and topcoats.

What paint finishes do you recommend for different rooms?

For living rooms and bedrooms, we recommend Matte or Eggshell finishes for easy spot cleaning without harsh glare. For kitchens, bathrooms, trim, doors, and baseboards, we recommend Satin or Semi-Gloss finishes for high moisture resistance and wipeability.

How do you price interior painting projects?

Here Handyman prices all jobs on a transparent flat-rate project basis. You receive one clear upfront quote before any work starts. Unlike corporate franchises, we never charge dispatch fees or hidden surcharges.
