Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle lead paint in older homes?

For homes built before 1978, we follow EPA lead-safe work practices. We test for lead paint and take appropriate safety measures including containment and proper disposal. For extensive lead paint removal, we may recommend certified lead abatement specialists.

Do you use kid-safe paints?

Yes - we use low-VOC and zero-VOC paints that are safe for children and pets. We can recommend premium brands like Benjamin Moore, Sherwin-Williams, and Behr that offer excellent coverage with minimal fumes.

How long does interior painting take?

A typical room takes 1-2 days including preparation, priming, and painting. Larger projects like full homes take 3-5 days depending on size and complexity. We provide accurate timelines during your free estimate.

Do you include wall preparation?

Yes - proper preparation is essential for flawless results. We include patching holes, sanding, cleaning, and priming as part of our painting service. This ensures the paint adheres properly and lasts longer.

Can you help me choose paint colors?

Absolutely. We can provide color consultations and help you select colors that complement your space. We also offer sample patches so you can see how colors look in your specific lighting before committing.

How much does interior painting cost?

Per-room painting starts at $299, 3+ rooms start at $799, and full home projects are quoted based on size. Contact us for a free, detailed estimate for your painting project.
