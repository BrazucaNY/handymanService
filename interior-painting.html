DIY vs. Hiring Here Handyman

Should you paint yourself or hire David?

🛠️ When You Can DIY:

Small touch-ups or painting a tiny accent wall with leftover paint are manageable DIY tasks if you have drop cloths, painters tape, and brushes on hand.

⭐ When to Call Here Handyman:

Full rooms, high ceilings, trim work, or color changes require extensive masking, patching, priming, and uniform roller coverage. Calling David avoids roller marks, paint drips, and multi-day exhaustion.
