Our 4-Step Service Process

01

Design & Layout Planning

We evaluate your bathroom space, measure plumbing lines, and assist with vanity and tile selection.

02

Demolition & Waterproofing

Removing old fixtures and installing cement board with premium waterproof membrane barriers.

03

Tile Setting & Vanity Install

Precision tile laying, grouting, setting new vanities, and connecting water supply lines.

04

Fixtures & Finishing Touch

Mounting mirrors, towel bars, glass doors, light fixtures, and applying 100% silicone seals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you install new bathroom vanities and sinks?

Yes, we assemble, position, level, and anchor bathroom vanities and connect faucet plumbing.

What waterproofing systems do you use behind shower tile?

We use industry-standard cement backer boards combined with liquid and sheet waterproof membranes to prevent moisture damage.

Can you replace an old toilet and install a new high-efficiency model?

Yes. We remove old toilets, replace worn wax rings and brass closet bolts, and install new toilets.

How long does a typical bathroom vanity update take?

A vanity and faucet swap typically takes 1 day, while full bathroom tile renovations are completed efficiently in several days.
