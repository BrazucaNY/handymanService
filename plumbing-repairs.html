Frequently Asked Questions

What plumbing repairs do you offer?

We handle faucet installation and repair, leak detection and repair, toilet fixes, drain clearing, garbage disposal repair, and minor pipe repairs. For major plumbing work like sewer line issues or water heater replacement, we recommend licensed plumbers.

Do you offer same-day plumbing repairs?

Yes - we frequently offer same-day plumbing repairs for minor issues throughout Westchester County. Call (516) 350-0801 to check availability for urgent plumbing needs.

Can you fix a leaking faucet?

Absolutely. We repair and replace all types of faucets including kitchen faucets, bathroom faucets, shower valves, and outdoor spigots. We can also install new faucets you've purchased.

Do you clear clogged drains?

Yes - we clear clogged sinks, showers, and tubs using professional drain clearing tools. For main line sewer backups or recurring drain issues, we recommend consulting with a licensed plumber.

How much does plumbing repair cost?

Every plumbing repair is unique. We provide free, upfront quotes tailored to your exact repair needs. Contact us today for a free quote.

Are you licensed plumbers?

We are skilled handymen who handle minor plumbing repairs and maintenance. For major plumbing work requiring a licensed plumber, we will be upfront and recommend the appropriate professional.
