DIY vs. Hiring Here Handyman

Should you attempt a plumbing fix yourself?

🛠️ When You Can DIY:

Replacing an aerator, tightening a loose shower arm, or swapping out a basic toilet flapper are quick DIY fixes if shut-off valves function properly.

⭐ When to Call Here Handyman:

Corroded shut-off valves, garbage disposals, or vanity faucet swaps require specialized basin wrenches, thread sealants, and leak testing. Calling David prevents unexpected water damage under your cabinets.
