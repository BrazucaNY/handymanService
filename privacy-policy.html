Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At Here Handyman ("we," "us," or "our"), respecting and protecting your privacy is a core priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (herehandyman.com), fill out a contact or booking form, or communicate with us regarding handyman services in Westchester County, NY.

1. Information We Collect

When you request a quote, book an appointment, or contact us through our website, we may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Contact Details: Name, phone number, email address, and home/service address.
  • Project Information: Service requested, project descriptions, preferred scheduling time windows, and photos uploaded of repair areas.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device details, and site usage data collected automatically via cookies and analytics scripts.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Providing accurate upfront project quotes and scheduling handyman service appointments.
  • Communicating with you regarding job status, appointment confirmations, and service follow-ups via phone, SMS, or email.
  • Improving our website performance, user experience, and local search visibility.
  • Complying with applicable legal, regulatory, and tax obligations.

3. SMS & Mobile Communications

If you provide your phone number, we may send you appointment reminders, text notifications, or quote updates. We strictly do not sell, rent, lease, or share your phone number or mobile opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

4. Analytics, Cookies & Advertising

We use standard analytics tools, including Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4 (GA4), to evaluate site traffic and user engagement. Cookies are small data files stored on your browser to enhance site performance. You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell your personal data to third-party data brokers. We may share necessary details with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website and delivering services (such as web hosting platforms and form processing services), provided they maintain strict confidentiality.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. All website communications are encrypted via HTTPS technology.
