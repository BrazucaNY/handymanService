Questions from Property Managers

Do you work with property managers on an ongoing basis?

Yes — we love working with property managers and landlords on a recurring basis. Once we complete your first job, we can set up a priority account so you get fast response times and consistent service across all your properties.

Do you provide invoices for accounting purposes?

Absolutely. We provide a detailed invoice for every job, broken down by service and labor, which makes it easy for your accounting team or property management software.

How fast can you respond for a tenant repair call?

We offer same-day and next-day availability throughout Westchester County 7 days a week. For property managers with recurring needs, we prioritize your calls to minimize tenant wait times.

Can you handle multiple units in the same building?

Yes — we regularly handle multi-unit jobs in a single visit. Whether it's 2 units or 10, we can coordinate the work efficiently so you're not scheduling multiple trips.

What types of tenant turnover work do you do?

Our tenant turnover services include drywall patching, touch-up painting, door and hardware fixes, light fixture replacement, furniture assembly, TV mounting, and general make-ready repairs — everything you need to get a unit rent-ready.

Do you serve all of Westchester County?

Yes — we serve all of Westchester County including White Plains, Yonkers, Scarsdale, New Rochelle, Tarrytown, Hartsdale, Harrison, Dobbs Ferry, Ardsley, Greenburgh, and surrounding areas.
