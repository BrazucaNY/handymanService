What We Do

Comprehensive Handyman Services

Knock out your home repair to-do list in a single visit. Led by 23+ year trade professional David, Here Handyman delivers fully insured, 5.0 ★ craftsman services across Westchester County, NY. Specializing in multi-item punch lists, furniture assembly, TV wall mounting, drywall patching, and fixture upgrades throughout Scarsdale, Chappaqua, Rye, Bedford, and White Plains with upfront flat-rate pricing.

Furniture Assembly

Professional assembly of couches, table units, beds, pergolas, gazebos, and expert backyard trampoline setups.

TV Mounting

Secure TV wall mounting on drywall or brick with in-wall wire hiding, and setup of Ring doorbells or smart lock systems.

Drywall Repair

Seamless sheetrock patching, wall texture matching, trim repairs, and water damage restoration.

Interior Painting

Flawless interior painting with premium finishes, clean execution, and meticulous wall preparation.

Plumbing Repairs

Faucet installation, leak repairs, toilet fixes, and minor plumbing maintenance throughout your home.

Smart Home Setup

Ring video doorbells, smart deadbolts, digital thermostat setup, keyless entry locks, and home automation device mounting.

Gutter Cleaning & Guards

Complete roofline leaf & sludge clearing, high-pressure downspout flushing, and stainless micro-mesh gutter guard installation.

Electrical & Lighting

Ceiling fan replacement, modern chandelier hanging, vanity bar lighting, recessed LED upgrades, and GFCI outlet safety repairs.

Door Repair & Locks

Pocket door alignment, storm door installation, weatherstripping, hinge adjustments, and premium hardware deadbolt replacements.

Handyman To-Do Lists

Knock out 5 to 10 household repairs in one visit: sticking doors, caulking, cabinet hardware, hanging heavy art/mirrors, and multi-item punch lists.
