Serving Greenburgh, NY

The Town of Greenburgh is one of Westchester's most complex municipalities, encompassing incorporated villages like Elmsford, Ardsley, and Dobbs Ferry, plus unincorporated hamlets including Hartsdale, Edgemont, Fairview, and Ardsley-on-Hudson. The town's residential areas are largely post-war construction - ranch homes, split-levels, and Capes built between 1950 and 1975 that are now entering a major maintenance cycle. Edgemont's highly rated school district draws new buyers into older homes needing updates. Here Handyman is a go-to resource for Greenburgh homeowners who want quality work at fair prices.
