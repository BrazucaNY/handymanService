Our 4-Step Service Process

01

Kitchen Consultation

We review your kitchen layout, select materials, and provide a clear, upfront estimate.

02

Demo & Surface Prep

Safe removal of old cabinets, worn countertops, and outdated tile backsplashes.

03

Cabinet & Tile Install

Leveling and mounting new cabinets, setting subway or mosaic tile, and installing sinks.

04

Hardware, Trim & Detail

Attaching cabinet pulls, installing under-cabinet lighting, painting trim, and final cleanup.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you install pre-assembled and RTA kitchen cabinets?

Yes! We install factory-built, custom, and flat-pack IKEA/RTA kitchen cabinets with solid wall leveling.

Can you install kitchen backsplash tile?

Yes. We tile subway, herringbone, glass mosaic, and stone tile backsplashes with clean grout lines.

How are licensed plumbing and electrical tasks managed for kitchen remodels?

David serves as your single point of contact and coordinates licensed master trade contractors whenever gas lines, main plumbing, or panel work is required.
