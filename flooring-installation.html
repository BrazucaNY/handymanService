Westchester Flooring Materials & Subfloor Engineering

Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP & SPC)

100% waterproof stone polymer composite (SPC) flooring ideal for busy Westchester kitchens, mudrooms, and finished basements. Rigid-core LVP withstands heavy foot traffic, pet claws, and moisture while delivering realistic wood grain finishes.

Subfloor Squeak Fixes & Leveling

A floor is only as good as the foundation beneath it. We address dipping joists, sand high plywood seams, pour self-leveling cement compounds, and drive heavy-gauge structural screws directly into joists to silence irritating floor squeaks forever.

Trim, Casings & Transition Molding

We undercut door casings and jambs so floor planks slide cleanly underneath without awkward gaps. We install new baseboards, shoe moulding, quarter round, flush stair nosings, and seamless doorway T-moldings for a luxury architectural finish.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of flooring do you install?

We install Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP), waterproof SPC vinyl, laminate, engineered hardwood, and ceramic/porcelain tile.

Can you fix subfloor squeaks before installing new floors?

Yes. We secure loose subfloor plywood to joists with heavy-duty screws to stop squeaks permanently.

Do you install baseboards and quarter-round trim with new flooring?

Yes, complete trim installation, caulking, and paint touch-ups are included.

How do I request a free flooring estimate in Westchester?

Call or text (516) 350-0801 or book online for a free, upfront estimate.

Can Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP) be installed over existing tile or concrete?

Yes. If existing tile is firmly bonded and grout lines are filled flat with leveling compound, rigid-core LVP can float directly on top. For basement concrete slabs, we test moisture levels and lay down a 6-mil poly vapor barrier.

How long should flooring materials acclimate prior to installation?

Engineered hardwood and laminate typically need 48 hours to acclimate to your home's indoor humidity and temperature. Rigid-core SPC vinyl requires minimal acclimation, allowing installations to proceed promptly without long delays.
