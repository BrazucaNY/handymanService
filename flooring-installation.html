Flooring Installation & Subfloor Repair

Durable, beautiful flooring installed flat and quiet. We install Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP), laminate, hardwood, and tile over properly prepped and leveled subfloors.

Book Appointment Online

Our 4-Step Service Process

01

Subfloor Inspection & Prep

We check subfloor flatness, fix squeaks, sand high spots, and apply leveling compound.

02

Underlayment & Acclimation

Laying moisture barriers and sound-dampening underlayment padding before flooring installation.

03

Precision Floor Laying

Staggering joints, maintaining expansion gaps, and locking click-lock or glue-down planks.

04

Baseboards & Transition Strips

Installing new baseboards, shoe molding, and doorway transition strips for a clean finish.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of flooring do you install?

We install Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP), waterproof SPC vinyl, laminate, engineered hardwood, and ceramic/porcelain tile.

Can you fix subfloor squeaks before installing new floors?

Yes. We secure loose subfloor plywood to joists with heavy-duty screws to stop squeaks permanently.

Do you install baseboards and quarter-round trim with new flooring?

Yes, complete trim installation, caulking, and paint touch-ups are included.
