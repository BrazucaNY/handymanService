Serving White Plains, NY

White Plains is Westchester County's seat of government and its most urban community. Downtown, office towers and residential high-rises surround the Galleria at White Plains and the City Center complex, while the Metro-North Harlem Line station carries tens of thousands of commuters each day. The Westchester County Center hosts trade shows, concerts, and civic events, and Westchester Medical Center anchors the city's eastern edge.

Away from downtown, White Plains is a city of distinct neighborhoods. Battle Hill and Chatterton Hill climb above the downtown grid with a mix of pre-war homes and newer construction, while Fisher Hill, Gedney Farm, and Green Acres offer the classic Westchester suburban streetscape. North White Plains and Saxon Woods round out the city's quieter corners.
