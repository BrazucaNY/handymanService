Frequently Asked Questions in Scarsdale

Common questions from homeowners regarding smart home installation in Scarsdale, NY.

Can you install a video doorbell on an antique Tudor door frame in Scarsdale?

Yes. We custom-fit Ring and Nest doorbells onto stone, brick, and carved wood trims, upgrading low-voltage doorbell transformers when needed to ensure reliable video feeds.

Will a smart deadbolt fit on an older custom front door in Scarsdale?

Yes. We adjust the door strike plate, mortise pockets, and latch alignment so smart locks (such as Schlage Encode or Yale Assure) lock effortlessly without jamming.

Can you replace multi-way switches with Lutron smart dimmers?

Yes. We install fixture-level smart switches and wireless Pico remotes to give you voice and smartphone control over chandelier and exterior lighting scenes.

Do you help connect devices to Apple HomeKit, Google Home, and Alexa?

Yes. Every smart home installation includes connecting your devices to your Wi-Fi network, configuring the smartphone app, and verifying your automation settings.
