Professional Deck, Patio & Siding Power Washing in Westchester NY

Westchester County’s freeze-thaw winter cycles and high summer humidity create the ideal environment for stubborn green algae, black mold, lichen, and mildew to coat exterior surfaces. Over time, slippery grime not only compromises your home’s curb appeal but can permanently stain stone patios, degrade composite deck coatings, and create slip hazards on exterior walkways and steps.

At Here Handyman, owner David delivers calibrated, surface-appropriate pressure washing and soft washing across Scarsdale, White Plains, Harrison, Rye, Tarrytown, and surrounding communities. We adjust water pressure and nozzle fan patterns specifically for each surface—restoring the natural beauty of wood, composite, and masonry without gouging wood fibers, stripping paint, or eroding mortar joints.

Exterior Surfaces We Clean & Restore

  • Bluestone & Flagstone Patios: Deep extraction of embedded moss, leaf tannins, and dirt from natural cleft bluestone, granite pavers, and fieldstone garden walls.
  • Composite & Natural Wood Decks: Low-pressure deep cleaning that lifts gray weather oxidization from Trex, TimberTech, and pressure-treated lumber in preparation for protective staining or sealing.
  • Paver & Concrete Driveways: High-pressure rotary surface cleaning that clears oil stains, tire marks, weed growth between pavers, and winter salt residue.
  • Vinyl & Cedar Shingle Siding: Gentle soft-wash applications that remove green algae and mildew spores along north-facing walls without forcing water behind exterior cladding.
  • Outdoor Furniture & Pool Coping: Cleaning teak patio sets, aluminum loungers, stone retaining steps, and poolside hardscapes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will high pressure damage my wood deck or siding?

Never. We use dedicated wide-angle fan tips and regulated PSI levels specifically calibrated for wood and siding to safely eliminate grime without etching, splintering, or scarring delicate surfaces.

Do I need to be home during the pressure washing service?

As long as exterior hose spigots have active water pressure and access gates are unlocked, you do not need to be home. David coordinates everything before arrival and sends completed job photos.

How is power washing priced?

We provide transparent, flat-rate quotes tailored to the square footage and surface condition of your project. Zero surprise fees, zero hidden add-ons.
