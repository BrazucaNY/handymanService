Frequently Asked Questions

Can you repair a door that sticks or won't latch shut properly?

Yes. Sticking doors are usually caused by loose hinges, settled frames, or humidity expansion. We tighten loose screw holes, plane edges accurately, and adjust strike plates so the latch catches cleanly.

Do you fix sliding closet doors that jump off their track?

Yes, we replace worn plastic rollers with heavy-duty metal ball-bearing rollers, level top tracks, and anchor floor guides securely.

Can you install smart locks and new deadbolts?

Yes, we install keypad deadbolts, smart door locks (Schlage, August, Yale), standard privacy knobs, and passage handles on interior and exterior doors.
